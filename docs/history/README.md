# Historical research records

These documents record completed diagnostics, earlier model variants and pilot configurations. Their commands, paths, figures and performance values describe the original experiment at the recorded date; they are not current operating instructions. Some referenced local experiments or retired scripts are intentionally excluded from the source release.

For the current Piper architecture, training schedule and entry points, use the root README, `docs/MODEL_CARD.md`, `docs/DATA_PROTOCOL.md` and `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`. Historical filtering policies, locks and RF2-PPI review/build records keep their established locations so their provenance remains intact.

The organizational cleanup manifest `docs/PIPER_CLEANUP_20261026.json` records renamed and retired paths. Compressed pre-cleanup sources remain locally under `artifacts/cleanup/`; they are not distributed as another active implementation.
